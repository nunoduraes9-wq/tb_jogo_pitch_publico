# Shattered Sky

**A co-op, turn-based squad RPG for Roblox where music is the magic system.**

> *Alone you’re a note. Together, a symphony.*

![The enemy roster](images/enemies-lineup.png)

Ten years ago a sound tore the sky open. Out of the rifts fall the **Dissonants**, creatures made of corrupted sound. A few people tuned in to that sound and became **Resonants**. You form a squad, step into the rifts, and fuse your attacks into **Chords**.

This repository is a public showcase of the project: the pitch, the design pillars and renders of the art. The game itself lives in a private repository.

## The idea in one mechanic

Every attack carries a **Tone** (Cut, Impact, Fire, Ice, Thunder, Void, Light). When teammates hit the same enemy one after another with *different* Tones, the chain builds:

| Chain | Name | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2 Tones | Interval | bonus damage |
| 3 Tones | Chord | a special effect that depends on the mix, and the chain plays a real musical chord |
| 4 Tones | Symphony | the whole squad lands one joint attack |

Everyone plans at the same time, reads the enemies’ intents, and agrees who strikes first with which Tone. It is turn-based, but nobody waits for their turn.

## Pillars

- **Solo or co-op, both complete.** The squad always has four seats. Solo, you command your hero and three Companions; with friends, they take the seats. Same game, same balance, same rewards.
- **Tactics over reflexes.** Planning together matters more than pressing buttons fast.
- **Identity from music.** Every character is an **Instrument** (class), a **Tone** (element) and a **Genre** (Metal, Classical, Jazz, Fado), and matching genres changes squad bonuses and the soundtrack.
- **Risk and reward.** At every rest room the squad votes: leave with the loot, or go deeper for more.
- **Fair.** Money buys cosmetics only. Never stats, techniques or ranks.

## Art

Everything is low poly but flowing, modelled in **Blender** and held to strict triangle budgets so the game runs on phones. The look is a stone **concert-hall city at blue hour**: warm stone, brass and lamplight, with colour reserved for Tones and rifts.

### The rifts

Each rift theme is a modular kit placed around the arena: a crystal cave where sound turned to stone, a drowned gothic cathedral, and a storm-powered foundry. Floors, water and lava are Roblox Terrain built in code.

### Instruments are weapons

Rifts drop gear in five tiers, from **Worn** to **Maestro**. The aura grows with every tier and takes the wielder’s Tone colour. Some drops are real instruments, one per class and Genre, and characters play them in battle.

![The same blade in five tiers](images/weapon-tiers.png)

![Instruments framed by their aura](images/instruments-aura.png)

![Top-tier weapons for each class](images/maestro-weapons.png)

The aura is never static: it is split into motion groups that the game animates every frame (flames flicker, ribbons spin, notes orbit), so the movement costs no extra triangles.

## How it is built

- **Roblox / Luau**, synced with **Rojo**; the combat engine is pure Luau with automated tests run in **Lune**.
- **Server-authoritative**: every action is validated on the server; player data is saved with session locking.
- **Art pipeline**: models are generated by Python scripts driving **Blender** through **blender-mcp**, exported as FBX with a manifest the game uses to place, scale and tint them. A build refuses any model over its triangle budget.
- **World**: the city, rifts and Haven are assembled in code; ground, water and lava are Roblox Terrain.

## Status

Playable core in development: the combat engine, squads, rifts, Companions and Haven are built; the art is being imported into Roblox next, followed by weapon drops, equipping and the instrument-playing animations.
